## 2. Verified product information

**Halad Special** (workbook, PDP sheet)
- Type: bio-stimulant
- Packaging: 5 litre can
- Dosage: 5 litres per acre
- Method of application: drenching or drip irrigation
- Description (verbatim from the client): "Halad Special is an advanced bio-stimulant formulated to support crop growth, stronger root development, improved stress tolerance, and yield and quality. Designed for application through drenching or drip irrigation, it helps deliver the product directly to the crop's root zone for effective uptake. Halad Special is suitable for farmers seeking a solution to strengthen plant performance throughout the crop cycle. Its formulation supports healthier plant development and efficient nutrient utilization, helping crops maintain growth under challenging conditions. With a recommended dosage of 5 Litre per acre, Halad Special is an easy-to-use addition to modern, scientific, and sustainable farming practices."
- Image: pack render embedded in the workbook (image10). It needs a final packshot.

## 8. Client feedback, 7 November 2026

The client returned the brand and mobile proposal with handwritten notes on pages 1 and 6 (`docs/planrequirement/`). Anything they did not mark stands as proposed:

- the direct relationship with farmers
- the five moments on phones (arrive, meet, choose, inspect, connect)
- WhatsApp, call and call back
- less dealer recruitment up front
- fewer metaphors about the ideal farmer
- short claims with proof

| Where | Client's note | What changed on the site |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Page 1, over "The brand idea" | "Lets understand farming togather / Learn farming together" | "Let's understand farming together." replaces "Your farm. Your decision. Oceania by your side." It is the tagline and the homepage headline. "Learn farming together" names the section for videos and guides (`/learn`) |
| Page 1, after "proud to participate" | "/ As educated, a learner" | Farmers are addressed as people who learn: the 14-line "Oceania farmer" section became three points, Learn, Ask and Share. The farmer community page now opens "For farmers who keep learning." |
| Page 1, "Confirmed offer" | "Farmer videos about farming" | Farmer videos are a new content type in the Studio (Learn, then Farmer videos), with their own page each so they can be shared on WhatsApp. Nothing is published until real videos with consent exist |
| Page 6, "More of this" | "Education / understanding" | Learning comes before products on the homepage. "Watch farmer videos" (or "Learn farming together" until videos exist) is one of the four paths |
| Page 1, underlined | "respected, confident and proud to participate"; "useful conversations, orders and field visits" | Kept as the measure of success. Two new kinds of enquiry, order requests and field visit bookings, join crop advice requests (the farm callback, renamed, with its own page at `/advice`). The staff area counts each kind for the last 30 days |

Decided with the developer on 7 November 2026:

1. Arial stays. The colours change to Pine and clay: pine `#24483C` for the brand, clay `#EDB58A` for actions, paper `#F5F1E8` for the page and ink `#1D2330` for text. An O-shaped frame, drawn from the letter O, is used around the hero photograph and on video play buttons. It is provisional until the official logo arrives.
2. "Let's understand farming together." is the brand line. "Learn farming together" names the learning section.
3. Ordering is an order request with no online payment. The team calls to confirm price and delivery.
4. The 14-line farmer section is replaced by the short "farmers who learn" section.

Removed from the homepage: the brand chapters, the partner band and the three "We..." statements. The statements are now on the About page, under the purpose.

New copy drafted for the client to approve is listed in `client-checklist.md` under Decisions.
