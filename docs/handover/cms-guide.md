# Updating the website: a guide for the Oceania team

This guide is for the people at Oceania who will keep the website up to date. You do not need any technical knowledge. Everything is done in the content editor (Sanity Studio) at:

    https://oceaniafertilizers.com/studio

Enquiries from the website are handled in a separate staff area, described at the end of this guide.

## 1. Signing in

1. Open the address above.
2. Sign in with the Google account or email address you were invited with. Ask the person who manages the website to invite you if you do not have access yet.
3. The menu on the left lists everything you can edit.

## 2. How publishing works

- **Every change starts as a draft.** Drafts are saved automatically as you type, and they never appear on the live website.
- **Publish** (bottom right) makes your changes live. The website updates within about a minute.
- **Preview** (the Preview tool at the top) shows a page with your unpublished changes, so you can check it before publishing.
- **Unpublish** (in the menu next to Publish) removes an item from the website but keeps it in the editor, so you can publish it again later.
- **History** (the clock icon) shows earlier versions. You can restore any of them.

Items that Oceania has not yet confirmed are already in the editor as drafts: six products, three articles, and the privacy policy and terms. They stay off the live website until someone checks them and presses Publish.

## 4. Common tasks

### Add or change contact details

Open **Site settings and contact details**, then the **Contact details** tab. Enter only details that are confirmed and ready to be public. Phone and WhatsApp numbers must be in the form `+919876543210`. Each detail appears on the website (footer, contact page and search results data) only once it is filled in. To hide a detail again, clear the field and publish.

### Edit a product

Open **Products** and choose the product. The fields are arranged in tabs:

- **Basics:** name, web address, category, the one-line purpose, the line printed on the pack, introduction and full description, pack sizes.
- **How to use:** dosage (amount, per what, and method), application methods, notes, suitable crops, practical guidance, and safety, handling and storage.
- **Features and benefits:** only claims that match the approved label and registration documents. Fill in **Claims approved by** and **Claims approved on** each time the claims change.
- **Images:** pack shots (with a description for each, see section 5), the background colour behind the pack shot, and the product leaflet (PDF).
- **Search:** optional title and description for Google.

Turn on **Feature on the homepage** to show a product in the homepage product section. Any section without content, for example dosage, is left off the product page.

### Publish one of the draft products

The six draft products (BINGOrhiza, Sanjivani N, JADUGAR, RUSTOM, NUTRISAC and MICRON) hold only a name and a one-line purpose taken from the design mockups. Before publishing one:

1. Check the name and purpose against the real product.
2. Add the description, pack sizes, dosage and method from the approved label.
3. Add a real pack shot.
4. Record who approved the claims, and when.
5. Publish.

### Add a farmer story

A farmer story is a short video of a farmer talking about their experience. The rules here protect the farmers and Oceania.

- **Consent first.** Tick **The farmer agreed to publication** only when the farmer has agreed, in writing or on record, to this being published. Enter the date, and note where the agreement is kept (for example, "Signed consent form, filed March 2026"). The editor will not let you publish without this.
- **Name to show:** exactly what the farmer agreed to: a full name, a first name with an initial, or a description such as "A turmeric grower".
- **Never enter phone numbers or home addresses**, in any field. A district and state are enough.
- **Video link:** upload the video to Oceania's YouTube (or Vimeo) channel first, then paste the link. Add English subtitles on YouTube where you can.
- **Short quote:** the farmer's own words from the video. Do not rewrite them.
- **Transcript:** what is said in the video, so that people who cannot hear it can still follow. Note the language.
- **Show on the website:** untick to hide a story without deleting it.

### Add a demo plot result

Fill in the crop, products used, state, district (the village is optional), season and year, and plot size. Two fields matter most:

- **Compared with:** what the comparison plot received, so the result means something.
- **What was observed:** what was seen, written as observations rather than promises. For example, "More even plant height across the plot", not "Increases yield by 30%".

Add before and after photos with the dates they were taken. Only use photos from the actual plot, and never generated or edited images. The same consent rules as farmer stories apply.

### Write or publish an article

Three starter articles are in the editor as drafts: on the Soil Health Card, mycorrhizal fungi and fertigation. Each lists its sources. Before publishing, an agronomist should read the article and check every statement. Record their name and the date in the **Review** tab. Then set the publication date and publish.

When you write a new article:

- Keep to what you can support, and list your sources.
- Link to the relevant products in **Related products**.
- Give every image a description.

### Add photos to the gallery

Use only Oceania's own photos, such as field visits, events and products. If a person can be recognised in a photo, you need their permission. Choose a category and drag to set the order.

### Update the privacy policy or terms

Both pages show a "pending legal review" note until a lawyer has reviewed them. After the review, make any changes, update **Last updated**, tick **Approved by legal review** and publish.

## 5. Images

- **Description for screen readers (required):** a short sentence saying what the photo shows, for someone who cannot see it. For example, "A turmeric field two months after planting, with drip lines between the rows." Do not start with "Image of".
- **Caption and credit:** the caption appears under the photo. For Oceania's own photos, credit "Oceania Fertilizers"; for others, the photographer and licence.
- **Crop and hotspot:** after uploading, click the crop icon and mark the important part of the photo. The website then keeps that part in view when it trims the photo for phones.
- **Size:** upload the original file, ideally 2,000 pixels or more on the long side. The website makes smaller versions automatically.
- **Honesty:** never present a generated or heavily edited image as a real field result.

## 6. Things the editor will not let you do, on purpose

- Publish a farmer story or demo plot without recorded consent.
- Save an image without a description.
- Use a reserved word (such as "category") as a product's web address.
- Duplicate or delete the fixed pages: homepage, about, partner programme, site settings, page openings and legal pages.

## 7. Enquiries: the staff area

Every form on the website (contact, product enquiry, farm callback, partner application, farmer registration) is saved, and the team receives an email for each one.

Sign in at `https://oceaniafertilizers.com/admin` with the staff password. The person who set up the website gives it to you; never share it by WhatsApp or email.

**Leads**
- The list can be searched by name, mobile, email or reference, and filtered by form, status and date.
- Open a lead to see everything that was sent. Set its status (New, Contacted, Qualified, Closed or Spam) and add internal notes. Only staff can see the notes.
- **Download CSV** gives you the current list in a spreadsheet.
- **Delete this lead** (at the bottom of a lead) removes it permanently. Use it when someone asks for their data to be erased, and when you clear out old records in the yearly review the privacy policy promises.

**Newsletter**
- Lists everyone who signed up in the website footer, and whether they are still subscribed.
- **Download subscribers (CSV)** gives the current list, with a personal unsubscribe link for each person. Put that link in every newsletter you send. Visitors who use it are removed automatically.
- If someone asks you directly to stop, press **Unsubscribe** next to their address.

**Looking after people's data**
- Contact people only about what they asked for. The lead shows whether they agreed to be contacted, and, for farmer registrations, whether they chose WhatsApp updates.
- Never add anyone to a WhatsApp group or mailing list unless they have agreed to it.
- Keep downloaded spreadsheets on work devices only, and delete them when you no longer need them.
- Requests about personal data (to see, correct or erase it) arrive through the contact form under "Privacy request". Reply to them promptly.

## 8. If something looks wrong

- **A change does not appear:** check that you pressed Publish, then wait a minute and reload the page.
- **A page shows a "Pending approval" marker:** you are looking at a preview copy of the website, not the live one. The live website never shows unapproved items.
- **No emails are arriving for new enquiries:** check the leads list. Every enquiry is there even if an email fails, and the "Email sent" column shows what happened. Tell the person who manages the website.
