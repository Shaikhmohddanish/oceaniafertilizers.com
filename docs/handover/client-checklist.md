# What we need from Oceania

The website is built and works. Wherever information is still missing, it hides that section or shows an honest placeholder, and it never shows invented details. This list covers what Oceania needs to supply or decide. Each item notes what the site does in the meantime.

Updated 7 November 2026 after Oceania's notes on the brand and mobile proposal. Items added or changed then are marked **(new)** or **(changed)**.

## Needed before launch

**1. Company details**
- [ ] Registered company name, and whether "Microbial Solutions Pvt Ltd, Hingoli" is connected to Oceania. A YouTube screenshot in the supplied files credits that company.
- [ ] The office address to publish. The mockups show two: Hingoli, Maharashtra, and a corporate hub in Ahmedabad, Gujarat.
- [ ] **(changed)** Phone number, WhatsApp business number and business hours. These now matter more than anything else on this list. On phones, a bar with WhatsApp, Call and Get advice stays at the bottom of every page. The homepage, the product pages, the advice page and the field visits page also offer WhatsApp and call. The numbers in the mockups are placeholders.
- [ ] The email address to publish. The mockups show both `info@oceania.co.in` and `info@oceaniafertilizers.com`, and the domain has no mailbox yet (see item 4).
- [ ] Links to social media accounts and the WhatsApp channel, if any.

- [ ] **(new)** The languages the team gives advice in. The site says "English and Marathi", taken from the founder brief in the proposal. It appears on the advice page and in the homepage's contact section, and on the contact page next to the phone and WhatsApp numbers.

*Meanwhile:* no address, phone or email appears anywhere on the site, and the bar on phones shows only Get advice. Every enquiry form works and is stored.

**2. Legal review**
- [ ] A lawyer to review the draft privacy policy (written for India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023) and the draft terms and conditions.
- [ ] A named person or contact for privacy requests and grievances.
- [ ] Confirmation of how long enquiry records are kept. The draft says records are reviewed every year.

*Meanwhile:* both pages carry a visible "draft pending legal review" note.

**3. Product facts**
- [ ] **Halad Special:** which crops it is for. The name suggests turmeric, but this is not stated anywhere in the supplied material, so no crops are shown.
- [ ] **WAVES:** dosage, how to apply it, which crops it suits, and confirmation that it is an Oceania product. The front of the pack does not show Oceania's name.
- [ ] For every product, its registration status under the Fertiliser Control Order and the approved label. Website claims must match the label.

- [ ] **(new)** Pack sizes for ordering. A product page shows the order form only when the product is published and has pack sizes. Today that is Halad Special (5 litre can, from the workbook) and WAVES (300 gm pouch, from the pack photo).

*Meanwhile:* only the facts given in the workbook and on the pack are published.

**4. Accounts and access**
- [ ] Accounts in Oceania's own name (the developer is added as a member):
  - Vercel (Pro plan)
  - Sanity
  - Neon
  - Resend
  - A mailbox provider such as Google Workspace or Zoho Mail
  - Google, for Search Console and, if wanted, Analytics
  - Cloudflare, if the spam check is needed
- [ ] Access to the GoDaddy DNS settings for `oceaniafertilizers.com`.
- [ ] Who should receive an email for each new enquiry.
- [ ] Who may sign in to the staff area to see enquiries.

`deployment.md` explains each service and the approximate monthly cost.

**5. Logo**
- [ ] The final logo as vector files (SVG or PDF), in colour and in white for dark backgrounds. The supplied files show four different logos. It needs to work on pine `#24483C` and on paper `#F5F1E8`, the site's new colours.
- [x] **(changed)** The tagline is settled: "Let's understand farming together." (Oceania's note on the proposal, 7 November 2026).

*Meanwhile:* a temporary typographic wordmark is used. Replacing it later takes one change. The O-shaped frame around the homepage photo and on video play buttons is drawn from the letter O. It will be redrawn to match the official logo.

**6. Orders, visits and advice (new)**
- [ ] Who handles each kind of request: crop advice, order requests and field visit bookings. The staff area counts each kind for the last 30 days.
- [ ] How the team confirms an order: the price, payment and delivery. The site takes no payment and shows no prices; it tells the farmer "Our team will call you to confirm the price and delivery."
- [ ] Which areas the team can visit for field visits and demo plots. The form accepts any state, and the site promises no coverage.
- [ ] Whether Oceania has agronomists who can be named as such. The proposal mentions agronomist visits; the site says "our team" until this is confirmed.

*Meanwhile:* every request is stored with a reference number and appears in the staff area. Email alerts start once the email service is set up (item 4).

- [ ] **(new)** A portrait of each person who will appear on the About page. The homepage now also shows up to three of them, under "The people you will talk to", until a farmer story is published.

*Meanwhile:* the site uses licensed photographs from Wikimedia Commons, credited under each photo and on the image credits page. The gallery stays empty until Oceania's own photos are added. Mockup images, AI-generated images and photos from other organisations are not used.

**9. Proof from the field**
- [ ] **Farmer stories:** videos of farmers who agree to be shown, each with:
  - written consent and its date
  - the name to show
  - district and state
  - crop and products used
  - English subtitles if possible
- [ ] **Demo plots:** for each plot:
  - crop, location, season and plot size
  - what was applied, and what the comparison plot received
  - dated before and after photos from the plot itself
  - what was observed, and the farmer's consent

- [ ] **(new)** **Farmer videos about farming**, the offer Oceania added on 7 November. These are short videos, by farmers or by the Oceania team, each with:
  - the video on Oceania's YouTube or Vimeo channel
  - a title, the language spoken, and a summary of one or two sentences
  - up to five points under "What you will learn"
  - an English transcript
  - the topic, the crop and the products shown
  - for anyone who appears: written consent and its date, the name to show, district and state

*Meanwhile:* the homepage leaves farmer stories and field results out, and their pages invite farmers to share their experience. The Learn page shows good farming practices and guides. The homepage path reads "Learn farming together" instead of "Watch farmer videos" until the first video is published. The names, quotes and results in the mockups are not used.

**10. About Oceania**
- [ ] The company story: when and why Oceania started, and where it works.
- [ ] Leadership: names, roles, short biographies and photos of the people who agree to appear.

*Meanwhile:* the About page shows a statement of purpose, a four-step approach and four principles. These were drafted from the brand copy for Oceania to approve (see Decisions). The story and leadership sections appear once content is added.

**11. Partner programme**
- [ ] The partnership types Oceania offers, what it looks for in partners, and what happens after someone applies.

*Meanwhile:* the page describes distributor, dealer, retailer and sales-partner roles in general terms, with no promises on margins or territories. The application form works.

**12. Articles**
- [ ] An agronomist to review the three starter articles (the Soil Health Card, mycorrhizal fungi and fertigation) and approve them, or suggest changes.

*Meanwhile:* the guides part of the Learn page waits for the first approved article.

## Decisions

- [ ] **Brand copy:** approve the lines taken from the mockups. The three "We..." statements are now on the About page. The 14 lines of the "Oceania farmer" section are no longer used (decided 7 November).
- [ ] **Drafted copy:** approve or change the text written for the site from the brand themes: the About page (purpose, approach, principles and vision), and the partner and farmer community pages. None of it states facts about the company beyond what was supplied.
- [ ] **(new)** **Copy drafted on 7 November** from Oceania's notes. Approve it or suggest changes. It can all be changed in the editor.
  - Homepage opening: "Let's understand farming together." and "Crop advice, quality inputs, field visits and farmer videos, direct from Oceania."
  - The four paths under "How can we help?":
    - Get crop advice: "Tell us your crop and the problem. Our team calls you back."
    - Order products: "Choose the product and the pack. We confirm the price and delivery."
    - Book a field visit: "A visit from our team, or a demo plot on your farm."
    - Watch farmer videos: "Learn from farmers who have tried it."
  - Learn section: "Learn farming together." and "Short videos and notes on soil, water, nutrition and crop care, from farmers and our team."
  - Farmers who learn: under "More than a farmer. A decision-maker.", the text "Oceania farmers learn before they decide. They watch their fields, ask questions and share what works." Then three points:
    - Learn: "Understand the soil and the crop before choosing an input."
    - Ask: "Check your doubts with our team before you spend."
    - Share: "Pass on what worked, and what did not."
  - Farmer community page: "For farmers who keep learning."
  - Advice page: "Get crop advice." and "Tell us your crop, where you farm and what you are seeing in the field. Our team will call you back with advice for your crop."
  - Field visits page: "Field visits and demos.", with three choices:
    - "A visit from your team to my farm"
    - "A demo plot on my farm"
    - "A visit to a demo plot near me"
- [ ] **(new)** **Test with farmers before adding more.** The proposal asks to check the site with a few farmers before expanding. `farmer-testing.md` describes a short test with 6 to 8 farmers.
- [ ] **Languages:** the supplied files include a Marathi version of the homepage. The site is ready for Marathi or Hindi pages whenever translations are available.
- [ ] **Login:** the workbook header shows a "Login" link. It has been left out because no dealer or partner portal exists yet.
- [ ] **Analytics:** whether to measure visits with Google Analytics. It runs without cookies, but the privacy policy must say so before it is switched on.
